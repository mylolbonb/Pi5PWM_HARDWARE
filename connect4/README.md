# Printable Connect 4

Every part fits a **250 × 250 mm** bed and prints **without supports**.
STEP files are in `step/`, STL files in `stl/`. The model is generated by `connect4.py` (CadQuery).

| File | Print | Size (mm) | Orientation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `frame_half_x2` | 2 | 250 × 200 × 6 | Plate face down, ribs up |
| `side_clamp_x2` | 2 | 90 × 10.2 × 8.1 | Flat back down, U opening up |
| `foot_x2` | 2 | 38 × 120 × 40 | Beam down (the shelf is a 7 mm bridge) |
| `release_bar_x1` | 1 | 250 × 32 × 3.8 | Lying flat |
| `disc_x42` | 21 + 21 | Ø29 × 3 | Flat |

**Estimated weight: about 190 g in total** (frame 83 g, discs 68 g, feet 28 g, clamps 8 g, bar 6 g). `weight.py` gives the per-part estimate for PLA with 2 walls and 15 % infill.
Suggested settings: PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers (the frame plates are 6 layers), 2 walls, 15 % infill.

## Clearances
- Disc in its column: 1.5 mm per side across the width, 0.75 mm per side front to back
- The disc overlaps each window by 2.5 mm at the top and bottom, so it can't fall out. The inner edges of the windows are chamfered so a falling disc can't catch on them.
- Pegs: Ø3.5 into Ø3.7 holes (snug press fit)
- Frame in the foot pocket: 0.15 mm per side
- `check_fit.py` checks the full assembly with all 42 discs loaded and finds no collisions between parts

## Assembly
1. Lay one frame half face down. Press the second half on top, turned around so its pegs (left wall) go into the first half's holes. The two halves are identical.
2. Push a **side clamp** sideways onto each side edge until it clicks into the 3 grooves. This locks the halves together.
3. Push the bottom corners of the frame into the two **feet**. The notch in each foot's end wall lines up with the bar slot.
4. Slide the **release bar** in from the right until its grip rests against the frame.

To empty the board, pull the grip to the right. The bar slides out through the notch and every disc drops out the bottom.

If a fit is too tight or too loose, change `CLR`, `PEG_HOLE_D` or `SNAP_H` in `connect4.py` and run `python3 connect4.py`.
